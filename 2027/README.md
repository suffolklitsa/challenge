## Moot court

- **Trial stage:** an evidentiary hearing, not a full trial. One expert witness per side, short direct and cross, then argument; about 40 minutes a round. The judge's short written ruling becomes the record.
- **Appeal:** each team appeals the ruling it actually received, or defends it.
- **Teams** of two or three. Closed universe: no outside research.

## Build challenge

Teams of three or four work on the fallout of the same case in one of six lanes ([lanes/](lanes/README.md)). Solo sign-ups are placed into teams. A submission doesn't have to be code: a memo with a decision tree, a workflow or agent design, a checklist or a guided interview all count. Every submission says where it fails.

Scoring: [rubrics/build.md](rubrics/build.md).
