# Rules (outline; full draft in progress)

These are the sections the 2027 rules will cover. Each will be filled in and reviewed before registration opens.

1. **Eligibility.** Who can compete, from which schools, and in which competitions.
2. **Teams.** Sizes for each competition; how solo sign-ups are placed; mixed-school teams.
3. **Closed universe.** What moot teams may and may not consult; what build teams may use.
4. **AI use and disclosure.** AI tools are allowed. Each team lists every tool it used and submits exported chats or transcripts with a short note on how it checked the output.
5. **Contact with judges and organizers.** None about the case outside official channels; questions answered publicly in this repo.
6. **Submissions.** What a build submission must include (see `tools/check_submission.py`), deadlines, formats.
7. **Scoring and tie-breaks.** As in the rubrics.
8. **Conduct.** Professional conduct; the Suffolk student code and the LITSA code of conduct apply.
9. **Accommodations.** How to request them, and by when.
10. **Data.** Everything in the case is fictional. No real client or patient data in any submission.
11. **Ownership.** Teams own their builds and grant LITSA a license to showcase them.
12. **Disputes.** How to raise a problem and who decides.
